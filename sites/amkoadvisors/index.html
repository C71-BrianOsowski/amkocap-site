Who We Are
An Independent Advisor on Your Side of the Table

As an independent municipal advisor, AMKO puts together the best possible financing structure and pricing for your project.

AMKO Advisors, LLC is a Registered Municipal Advisor with the Municipal Securities Rulemaking Board (MSRB) #K0852 and the United States Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) #867-00428. We do not underwrite or purchase the obligations we advise on, so our only interest in a transaction is yours.

From our office in Fargo, North Dakota we serve communities across the Upper Midwest and beyond, working alongside city councils, school boards, county commissions, water resource districts and state agencies through every stage of a financing and the years of administration that follow.

01
Independent
No underwriting desk, no bank affiliation. Our advice is not tied to who buys the bonds.
02
Fiduciary

What We Do
Services
AMKO offers a step-by-step process toward identifying your financing needs, then stays with you through issuance, ongoing compliance and the management of the debt and deposits that result.
01
Debt Issuance
Structuring, sizing and bringing bonds, notes and certificates to market. We prepare the plan of finance, coordinate bond counsel, rating agencies and paying agents, run the competitive sale or negotiation, and manage closing.
02
Debt Management
Ongoing review of outstanding obligations for refunding and restructuring opportunities, debt capacity analysis, and the records, schedules and reports a governing body needs to manage its debt portfolio with confidence.
03
Deposit & Investment Management
Advice on the deposit, collateralization and investment of bond proceeds, reserve funds and operating balances so that public funds are safe, liquid and earning what they should.
04
Continuing Disclosure
We will work with you and your community to ensure your existing bonds' disclosure requirements are completed timely and accurately, including annual filings and event notices on the MSRB's EMMA system.
Filing assistance via AMKO Bond Services
05
Leasing & Payment Processing
Tax-exempt lease financing for equipment, technology and infrastructure through the AMKO Lease Advantage, and credit card and ACH payment processing for municipal entities, online, by phone and on site.
Delivered through AMKO Capital
06
Economic Development
Financing tools for growth: tax increment and special assessment districts, revenue bonds, private activity bonds for qualifying private projects, utility rate analyses and the fiscal impact studies that let a board decide with the numbers in front of it.
How a Financing Comes Together
A Step-by-Step Process
Every issue is different, but the path from an identified need to a closed financing follows the same disciplined sequence. We lead each step and keep your staff and governing body informed throughout.
01
Together
Identify the Need
We meet with staff and the governing body to define the project, timing, revenue sources and legal authority available to fund it.
02
AMKO
Structure & Plan of Finance
Sizing, term, security, call features and rating strategy are modeled and compared so the structure fits your budget and your policy.
03
AMKO
Documents & Disclosure
We coordinate bond counsel, prepare the Official Statement and terms of offering, and assemble the financial and demographic disclosure investors expect.
04
Market
Sale & Award
Bonds are sold competitively or negotiated with a purchaser. We evaluate bids, verify the true interest cost and recommend the award to the governing body.
05
Ongoing
Closing & Administration
Proceeds are delivered and invested, the final Official Statement is posted, and continuing disclosure and debt service are tracked for the life of the issue.
AMKO Advisors acts solely as municipal advisor to its clients. We do not underwrite, purchase or place securities, and the fiduciary duty we owe runs to the issuer alone.
