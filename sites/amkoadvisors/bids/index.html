    How bids are ranked
    Bids are ranked by True Interest Cost (TIC): the annual rate, compounded semiannually, that discounts every principal and interest payment back to the dated date so that their present value equals the issuer's net proceeds (par less your one-time fees). The lowest TIC is recommended for award. Net Interest Cost, total interest and total debt service are shown for reference. A lower quoted rate does not necessarily win if it carries higher fees.
